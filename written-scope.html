The Written Scope

Every paid door starts with a short written scope. It says what you get, what it costs, and what stays sealed. Nothing is charged until you agree to it in writing.

Four steps

1. Brief

Use the booking page or email vault@obscurai.io with your question and data description.

2. Scope

You receive the written scope. Ask for changes until it is right.

3. Agree

Only after you agree in writing is anything charged or invoiced.

4. Deliver

Results and a report, as scoped. Methods stay sealed.
